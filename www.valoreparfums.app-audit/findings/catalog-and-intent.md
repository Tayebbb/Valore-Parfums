**Content evidence.** On the live decant landing page, affordable landing page and Lattafa article, all 12 distinct long body paragraphs were shared. Each page rendered 23 occurrences. The local versions share zero long paragraphs across those three samples. The Lattafa and winter guides link matching catalog records. Five buying guides are now linked from the blog index. The fragrance name supplies concentration only where the catalog records it. No concentration, wear-time guarantee, ratings or identifier was inferred from a brand or bottle image. **Offer evidence.** Live Hawas Ice exposed 5 ml at BDT 259 as the selected price even when opened at its 10 ml URL. It also published a full-bottle preorder beside a disabled ordering control and advertised a seven-day return policy, fixed validity dates and a BDT 80 nationwide delivery rate. The checkout configuration returned BDT 80 inside Dhaka and BDT 120 outside. A customer can ask about a bottle separately. The enquiry does not supply a confirmed price or reservation. The fixed markup omits those unsupported promises and groups the actual size-specific Product and Offer nodes by catalog ID. The entire variant list is intentionally stable as a shopper selects among unchanged offers. Each child has its own size, SKU, destination and price. A stock or price refresh updates that same list and the visible controls. **Remaining gaps.** The configured Niche filter returned zero active matches while the store links to it. Confirm the group membership or assortment before promoting it. Some fragrance names omit concentration. Ask the merchant to verify those against original packaging. Archive confirmed shipping times, returns eligibility and any identifiers before adding merchant policies or identifiers to markup. Product galleries use actual catalog images; decorative collage tiles have empty alt text beside the constituent fragrance names. Add new product photos only where original imagery is available.
